简介 About

刘志豪，香港大学电气工程与计算机博士研究生，导师为齐晓娟教授，所在实验室为计算机视觉与机器智能实验室（CVMI Lab）。预计 2030 年 12 月毕业。2026 年本科毕业于南京理工大学飞行器设计与工程专业。

Zhihao Liu is a Ph.D. student in the Computer Vision and Machine Intelligence Lab (CVMI Lab) at The University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Xiaojuan Qi. He is pursuing a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering, expected to graduate in December 2030. He received his bachelor's degree in Aerospace Design and Engineering from Nanjing University of Science and Technology in 2026.

博士前，他曾在阿里云、字节跳动、MiniMax、StepFun 和 NaiveAI 从事容器云、后端服务、Agent 系统及模型后训练基础设施相关工作，目前在 NaiveAI 负责强化学习基础设施。个人研究兴趣包括分布式系统与云计算，涵盖虚拟化、沙箱隔离以及大规模强化学习系统。

Before his doctoral studies, he worked on container cloud, backend services, agent systems, and post-training infrastructure at Alibaba Cloud, ByteDance, MiniMax, StepFun, and NaiveAI. He currently works on reinforcement learning infrastructure at NaiveAI. His own research interests lie in distributed systems and cloud computing, including virtualization, sandbox isolation, and large-scale reinforcement learning systems.
